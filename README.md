## 🚀 Overview
A high-performance, dark, technically rigorous personal engineering portfolio built for **Venkatesh C L** (Robotics & Automation Engineer). Designed as a mission control center with high-contrast telemetry, command prompt typography (`Consolas`, `Lucida Console`), skippable terminal boot animation, and an interactive **VENKY AI Voice Assistant** that speaks, listens, and navigates visitors through verified engineering systems.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Framework:** React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Custom CMD Monospace Font Stack
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: `#0B0F1A` (Kali Deep Obsidian)
  - Primary Accent: `#00D4FF` (Cyan Glow / Telemetry)
  - Secondary Accent: `#007BFF` (Electric Command Blue)
  - Surface Panels: `#101B2B` & `#14243A`
  - High-Contrast Text: `#EAF5FF`
- **Voice & Audio:** Browser-native **Web Speech API** (SpeechRecognition + SpeechSynthesis) with graceful typed text fallback
- **Icons:** Lucide React & Handcrafted Technical SVGs

## 🎙️ Interactive Voice Assistant (VENKY AI)
VENKY AI guides recruiters and visitors through the portfolio with both voice recognition and voice readouts:
- **Speech Recognition:** Click the pulsing circular waveform or microphone to speak naturally.
- **Voice Synthesis:** VENKY AI speaks responses out loud using synthesized speech (with quick Mute / Voice On controls).
- **Interactive Navigation Guidance:** When asked to view projects, work experience, or contact, VENKY AI automatically smooth-scrolls and highlights the relevant section.
- **Strict Verification Boundary:** VENKY AI only speaks verified facts from Venkatesh's approved dossier. If asked an out-of-scope question, it politely offers confirmed contact channels.

### Quick Voice / Text Commands:
- `"Tell me about Venkatesh"` → High-level background & navigates to **About Me**
- `"Show me his projects"` → Outlines major systems & navigates to **Projects**
- `"Tell me about the underwater robot"` → Deep dive & opens the **Submersible Micro Robot** dossier
- `"Explain his work experience"` → Details 8 months internship & navigates to **Experience**
- `"What are his skills?"` → Hardware, PLC, CAD, and tools matrix
- `"Tell me about his education"` → B.E. (CGPA 7.8) and Diploma in EEE (75.40%)
- `"Open contact section"` → Direct phone, email, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn
